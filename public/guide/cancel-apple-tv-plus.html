How to Cancel Apple TV+ UK

← Back to App  ·  Subscription Guide  ·  Cancel Apple Music  ·  Published March 2026

Apple TV+ is Apple's video streaming service offering original films and series. Before cancelling, check whether you are subscribed directly or as part of an Apple One bundle — the cancellation process differs, and Apple One cancellation affects multiple services at once.

Cancelling on iPhone or iPad

  1. Open Settings and tap your name at the top.
  2. Tap Subscriptions.
  3. Find Apple TV+ (or Apple One) and tap it.
  4. Tap Cancel Subscription and confirm.

Cancelling on Mac

  1. Open the App Store and click your name in the bottom-left.
  2. Click View Information at the top of the page.
  3. Scroll to Subscriptions and click Manage.
  4. Find Apple TV+ and click Edit, then Cancel Subscription.

Cancelling via the Web

  1. Go to appleid.apple.com and sign in.
  2. Under Subscriptions, click Manage.
  3. Find Apple TV+ and select Cancel Subscription.

Key Points to Know

Apple One subscribers

If you subscribe through Apple One, you cannot cancel Apple TV+ on its own — cancelling Apple One ends all bundled services (Apple Music, iCloud+, Arcade, and others depending on your tier). Consider downgrading your Apple One plan instead.

Billing and access

You keep access to all Apple TV+ content until the end of your current billing period. No partial refunds are issued for unused time.

Downloaded content

Any content downloaded for offline viewing will become unavailable once your subscription expires.

Family Sharing

If you share Apple TV+ with family members through Family Sharing, cancelling removes access for all members when the subscription ends.
