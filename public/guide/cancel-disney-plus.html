How to Cancel Disney+ UK

← Back to App  ·  Subscription Guide  ·  Cancel Netflix  ·  Cancel Amazon Prime

If you're looking to cancel your Disney+ subscription in the UK, the process is straightforward and can be done through the website or directly in the mobile app. This guide covers the steps for both methods and explains what happens to your account after you cancel.

Cancelling Disney+ on the Web

  1. Go to disneyplus.com and log into your account.
  2. Click on your profile icon and select Account.
  3. Under the Subscription section, click Cancel Subscription.
  4. Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm cancellation.

Cancelling via the Mobile App

  1. Open the Disney+ app on your device.
  2. Tap your profile icon and go to Account.
  3. Select Manage Subscription.
  4. Tap Cancel Subscription and confirm your choice.

Key Points to Know

Billing cycle

After cancelling, you retain access to Disney+ until the end of your current billing period. Your subscription will not renew after that date. Disney+ does not offer partial refunds for cancellations mid-cycle.

Subscribed via Apple or Google Play?

If you originally subscribed through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store, you must cancel through those stores' subscription settings — not the Disney+ website. On iPhone, go to Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions. On Android, open the Play Store and go to Subscriptions.

What you lose on expiry

Once your subscription expires you lose access to all content and your watchlist. Profile data may be deleted after a period of inactivity if you do not resubscribe.
